# Scope

## Question

What exact fail-closed receipt contract must bind the seven original-client movement anchors to one fresh run, one ordered outbound `0x0B01` / inbound `0x0B07` correlation, and owned-HWND before/after evidence before any live launch?

## Included

- Offline static anchor ledger for primary MVB01-MVB07 plus queue-completion MVB08-MVB09.
- JSON Schema and empty not-live template.
- Semantic verifier and mutation tests.
- Prelaunch v6 integration that replaces the missing-schema blocker with a fresh live-install receipt blocker.
- Independent read-only review, evidence closure, handoff, and bounded commit.

## Excluded

- VM lifecycle or guest operation.
- x32dbg attach, breakpoint installation, process-memory access, input, or screen capture.
- Binary/resource patch, process-memory write, automatic input/retry, or server/protocol/database change.
- Permit issuance or reuse of the consumed permit.

## Acceptance

- Exact module-relative anchors and ordered semantics are hash-bound, including expected-opcode comparison and dequeue completion.
- Empty template contains no run-specific values and cannot self-promote.
- A hand-authored synthetic specimen proves the verifier can accept the intended structure without becoming runtime evidence.
- Mutations of identity, anchors, hit order, opcodes, correlation, pixels, operations, authority, or permit state fail closed.
- Prelaunch remains not ready and retains the activation-budget mismatch.
